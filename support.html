Common questions

How do I restore Crane Pro on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Google account you subscribed with, open Crane PDF, open the Crane Pro screen and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel?

Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Crane PDF, Cancel subscription. You keep Crane Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

The app says to update Google Play.

Your Play Store app is too old to sell subscriptions. Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Settings, About and Update Play Store.

Some of my PDF files are not listed.

Add the folder that holds them in the library. Crane only lists folders you have granted.

Can I change my ad consent choice?

Yes. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, go to Profile and tap Privacy options.
